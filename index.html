What this is

The whole pitch

Every profile picture on your timeline is doing work for someone. An ape, a punk, a cat — it says something before a word gets typed. BoxHead is what's left when you stop trying so hard.

A cardboard box, eyes half shut, ink running where a face should be. It isn't hyped about the chain, the cycle, or itself. That's the character, and that's the whole personality of the collection.

3,333 of them on Robinhood Chain. Small supply on purpose — I'd rather sell fewer and not have to convince anyone.

Holders get full commercial rights to their own head. Use it, print it, put it on something. It's yours.

Holder raffle

Free to enter — always

How it works

  1. Hold a BoxHead. That's the entry. Nothing is sent, burned, locked, or staked.
  2. A snapshot is taken on the date posted on X. One head held equals one entry.
  3. Two winners are drawn. The draw and the transaction hashes get posted publicly, every round.

Entry — not open

Read-only when it opens. This site never requests a transaction, a signature, or a seed phrase. If any page claiming to be BoxHead asks for one, it isn't this one.

What I'm not promising

Read this part twice
Token
Staking rewards
A game
Metaverse land
Passive income
A 5-phase roadmap

Every one of those is easy to type and almost impossible to deliver alone. I'd rather under-promise and still be here in six months than post a roadmap I quietly delete later.

What you get is the art, the commercial rights to it, a raffle I fund myself, and someone who posts the work as it happens — including the parts that go wrong.
